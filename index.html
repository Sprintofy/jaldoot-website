Welcome to Jaldoot

What is Jaldoot Water Conditioner?

Jaldoot Water Conditioner / Softener is an advanced water treatment system combining Ultrasonic + Magnetic Static + Pulsing Power + Catalytic Technology, controlled by a microprocessor — treating hard water without chemicals, salt, or maintenance.

Ultrasonic Magnetic Static Pulsing Power Catalytic

No Chemicals

Works without any chemicals, salt, or consumables — completely natural water treatment.

Zero Maintenance

Install once and forget — no filters to change, no salt to add, no recurring maintenance costs.

Scale Reduction

Converts hard scale-forming minerals into harmless 2–4 micron crystals that don't stick to surfaces.

Long-Lasting Effect

Treated water retains its conditioned properties for up to 7 days as stated by the company.
