Company Introduction

Krushi Nature Water Care Pvt. Ltd.

Krushi Nature Water Care Pvt. Ltd. is an ISO 9001:2008 certified company engaged in the manufacturing and supply of advanced water conditioning solutions. The company specializes in Magnetic, Electro Magnetic, Electronic, Pulse, Water Conditioner/Softener, De-Scalar and Catalytic Technology products.

The company's flagship product, Jaldoot Water Conditioner, represents the culmination of advanced research in water treatment technology. It combines Ultrasonic, Magnetic Static, Pulsing Power and Catalytic Technology in a single microprocessor-controlled system.

Jaldoot technology transforms hard water problems into lasting solutions — converting hardness-causing minerals into harmless 2–4 micron crystals without using chemicals, salt, or requiring ongoing maintenance.

Our Technology

Jaldoot Technology

Four powerful technologies combined in one microprocessor-controlled system for comprehensive water conditioning.

01

Ultrasonic

High-frequency wave technology

Ultrasonic waves break down hard water mineral bonds at a molecular level. This first line of treatment initiates the conversion process, disrupting calcium and magnesium compound structures to begin transformation into harmless particles.

Stage 1 Treatment
02

Magnetic Static

Permanent magnetic field

A permanent magnetic field alters the crystalline structure of calcium carbonate from its hard, scale-forming calcite form into a softer aragonite form that does not adhere to pipe surfaces and equipment.

Stage 2 Treatment
03

Pulsing Power

Variable frequency signals

The microprocessor generates variable-frequency electronic pulses through coil cables. These signals create an oscillating electromagnetic field preventing mineral crystallization and scale formation throughout the pipe system.

Stage 3 Treatment
04

Catalytic Technology

Crystal conversion process

Catalytic conversion transforms remaining hardness minerals into stable, harmless micro-crystals of 2–4 micron size. These crystals remain suspended in water and flow freely. The conditioning effect lasts up to 7 days.

Stage 4 Treatment

Microprocessor-Controlled System

The entire Jaldoot system is controlled by an advanced microprocessor that automatically manages and optimizes all four technologies — Ultrasonic, Magnetic Static, Pulsing Power and Catalytic — ensuring consistent, reliable performance across all water conditions and flow rates.
